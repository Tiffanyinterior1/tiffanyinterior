About

I am an interior designer drawn to exploring the dynamics of design and how spaces can be tailored to reflect unique needs and personalities of each client. I am passionate about creating intimate environments that are both functional and emotionally resonant, using materials, colours and sensory elements to evoke a deep connection with the space and the client. Sustainability is a key focus in my work. I believe it is essential to design with an awareness of the environment, finding ways to integrate aspects into every project. Additionally, as someone who is half Cantonese and half English, I am inspired by cultural diversity and how traditions and aesthetics can inform yet also elevate a design.
